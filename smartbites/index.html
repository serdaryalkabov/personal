How it works

Four simple steps to complete nutritional awareness

01

Set Your Preference

During onboarding, choose your estimation bias. From "Underestimate More" to "Overestimate More," you're in control of how the AI rounds its calculations.

02

Capture Your Meal

Take a photo of your food. Our AI instantly recognizes ingredients, estimates portion sizes, and provides a detailed breakdown.

03

Refine the Details

Review and edit everything. Adjust weights, add missing ingredients, remove incorrect items, or change the description. The AI recalculates automatically.

04

Track & Save

View your complete nutritional data. Save frequent meals to your library for instant tracking next time.
